# Superlite Privacy Policy

Publisher: Iheatu Wogu

Superlite does not collect personal data. The publisher does not receive, share, or retain personal data through Superlite, so there is no publisher-held personal data from Superlite to delete.

Superlite runs through the coding-agent host you choose. Its workflows can read and change files in your project and create local plans, progress records, and other working files. Those files remain subject to your project and host settings. You can remove Superlite-generated local files when you no longer need them.

Your coding-agent host and any external services you choose to use process information under their own policies. This policy covers Superlite, not those separate services.

For questions about Superlite, use [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/iha2/superlite/issues). Issues are public; do not include passwords, private source code, or personal information.
