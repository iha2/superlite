# Superlite - Contributor Guidelines

Read and follow [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md) before doing anything in this repository.

Under Claude Code, `EnterWorktree name=` and `path=` are authorised for the `session: create EnterWorktree name=<n>` and `session: enter EnterWorktree path=<abs>` lines superlite prints (also `proto: create`), and `ExitWorktree` to leave or remove them (see AGENTS.md, "Worktrees under Claude Code").
